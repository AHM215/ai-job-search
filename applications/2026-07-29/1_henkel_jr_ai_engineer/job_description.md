## Role Summary

Henkel is hiring an AI engineer within Global Business Solutions + IT in Cairo. The role focuses on enterprise AI delivery: building agentic LLM applications, RAG pipelines, AI integrations into business interfaces and enterprise data systems, and responsible AI practices.

## Core Responsibilities

- Build agentic LLM applications with tool-calling, routing, and multi-step orchestration.
- Build RAG pipelines over enterprise documents and data.
- Define evaluation criteria such as accuracy, groundedness, cost, and latency.
- Integrate AI into business interfaces such as Teams, chat, and voice.
- Develop scalable AI pipelines and integrate models into enterprise systems.
- Evaluate use cases, model performance, and business impact.
- Implement responsible AI, governance, security, and monitoring practices.
- Optimize AI solutions for scalability, reliability, and operational efficiency.

## Stated Requirements

- LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG architecture
- Azure
