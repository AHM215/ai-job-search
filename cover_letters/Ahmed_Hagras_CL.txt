\section{WHY I WANT TO BUILD AI AT SIEMENS}

\small{
I'm a Python developer who works on Large Language Models, Machine Learning, and
full-stack web applications, and I care about the part that comes after the demo:
getting an AI system to actually hold up in production.

\vspace{8pt}
Right now, I build agentic systems in production, including one that serves users
at scale for one of Egypt's most popular e-commerce brands, connected across its
Meta channels (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram) and a website widget. I own it end
to end --- design, orchestration logic with LangGraph, deployment, and the
monitoring that keeps it reliable once real people are using it every day. That's
the same concept-to-deployment ownership this role is asking for, and it spans
both the LLM/ML side and the backend and web development side, which is exactly
the mix Siemens is looking for.

\vspace{8pt}
Alongside that, I've built a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline
indexing over \textbf{50,000 documents} with under \textbf{200\,ms} latency, and
deployed FastAPI web services and APIs on AWS ECS using Docker containers. I also
work with Machine Learning and Deep Learning libraries such as scikit-learn and
PyTorch, and I'm comfortable picking up new frameworks (Keras, Kubernetes,
ReactJS/Angular) when a project calls for it.

\vspace{8pt}
What draws me specifically to Siemens EDA is the scale and rigor of the problem:
software that engineering teams around the world rely on to design chips, boards,
and systems. That's a higher bar than most products get held to, and it's the
kind of environment I want to grow in --- owning features from design and code
review through testing and release, alongside strong technical peers.
}
